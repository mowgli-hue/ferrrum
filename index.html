03 / The shop

Metal,
measured twice.

Ferrum is an exterior sheet metal contractor based in Langley Township, BC. We fabricate and install metal cladding and metal roofing systems — from a single custom flashing to full architectural panel packages on commercial builds.

We work directly with homeowners, builders, general contractors, architects, and roofers across the Lower Mainland.

/01

Built to drawing

Send us prints, sketches, or measurements off the wall — we'll fabricate to your spec, not a stock catalogue.

/02

Trades-grade tolerances

Tight bends, clean welds, square corners. Our work fits the first time so installers aren't fighting it on site.

/03

Local turnaround

Serving Langley, Surrey, Abbotsford, and the Lower Mainland — quick quotes, faster lead times than out-of-region shops.

/04

Materials that last

Galvanized, stainless, aluminum, and coated steels — gauged correctly for the job and the BC weather it has to survive.

04 / Workflow

How a job moves through the shop.

01

Quote

Send drawings, photos, or rough dimensions. We come back with scope, materials, and pricing — no guesswork.

02

Detail

Final measurements, panel layouts, and shop drawings confirmed before a single sheet gets cut.

03

Fabricate

Cut, brake, seam, and finish in our Langley shop. Quality-checked piece by piece, not at the end.

04

Install

On-site install by our crew, or supplied to your installer. Crated, labelled, and ready to go up.
