02 / The shop

Metal,
measured twice.

Ferrum is a working sheet metal shop based in Langley Township, BC. We fabricate to spec — from a single bracket to full HVAC packages for new construction. No middlemen, no off-the-shelf compromise. Drawings come in, finished metal goes out.

We work directly with homeowners, builders, mechanical contractors, architects, and roofers across the Lower Mainland.

/01

Built to drawing

Send us prints, sketches, or a measurement off the wall — we'll fabricate to your spec, not a stock catalogue.

/02

Trades-grade tolerances

Tight bends, clean welds, square corners. Our work fits the first time so installers aren't fighting it on site.

/03

Local turnaround

Serving Langley, Surrey, Abbotsford, and the Lower Mainland — quick quotes, faster lead times than out-of-region shops.

/04

Materials that last

Galvanized, stainless, aluminum, and coated steels — gauged correctly for the job and the weather it will live in.

03 / Workflow

How a job moves through the shop.

01

Quote

Send drawings, photos, or rough dimensions. We come back with scope, materials, and pricing — no guesswork.

02

Detail

Final measurements, bend allowances, and shop drawings confirmed before a single sheet gets cut.

03

Fabricate

04

Deliver

Crated, labelled, and dropped to site or pickup ready. Installation support a phone call away.
